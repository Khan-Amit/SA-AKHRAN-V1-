## 🌾 Philosophy (The Village Well)

When floodwaters rise, villagers do not build a larger pipe. They build a filter.

1. **Collect** – Water from the river (raw data).
2. **Settle** – Earthen pots remove mud and silt (pattern matching).
3. **Disinfect** – Alum and boiling (stateful inspection, encryption).
4. **Consume** – Clean water (essential data only).

SA‑AKHRAN applies the same wisdom to bytes. No waste. No noise. No compromise.
